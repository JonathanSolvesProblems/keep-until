# Found details: the food bank sort world

Sources: Wikimedia Commons photos of a food bank's sort line and warehouse
(`food-bank-sorting-volunteers-1..4`, `food-bank-warehouse-0..4`), and my own pantry photos with
real Canadian date stamps (`web/public/testset/pantry-2026-11-15/`). The other Commons results
(`best-before-date-can-*`, `food-drive-donations-canned-0/3/4`) were off-topic and are not used.

## The place
- Long black sort tables (#0e0e10, #211d17) with kraft boxes standing open on them, one box per
  destination. Volunteers stand on both sides and drop each item into a box.
- Every volunteer wears cobalt-blue nitrile gloves. The only saturated blue in the room, and it is
  always on the hands holding the food.
- Shrink-wrapped flats of cans stacked in rows: silver lids in a grid, labels in every colour,
  cardboard trays under them.
- Racking: orange beams (#ce813f, #c37939, #965223) on teal-blue uprights (#2c4c5f), yellow
  safety bollards and yellow floor striping (#d7be68, #e5cf98).
- White painted block walls (#d2ccc6, #beb6b1, #e6e0d7) under cold high-bay light; floor polished
  grey concrete with a sheen.
- Banners on the walls in flat bright panels; handwritten marker on boxes.

## The stamp (the detail no model would invent)
- The date on a can is a black dot-matrix inkjet stamp, two lines, often upside down and slightly
  smeared, printed straight onto a silver lid with concentric rings (#bab8b4 on #0e0e10 shadows).
- On a carton it is printed on white in the same dot matrix: "BEST BEFORE / MEILLEUR AVANT" in
  tiny caps, then "2027 FE 14 02146" with the lot code running on after the date.
- Canadian stamps are bilingual and year-first with a two-letter month (JA FE MR AL MA JN JL AU SE
  OC NO DE). The month code MA is May and also the "meilleur avant" in BB/MA.
- Expiry dates (formula, Ensure) say EXP; packaged-on dates say "Packaged on / Empaqueté le".

## The decision
- The sort is three physical places: the keep box, the discard box, and the lead's table.
- Food Banks Canada's chart is a printed grid of rows (product type) by columns (room temperature,
  refrigerated, frozen) with a time past best before in each cell, colour-banded by food group.
