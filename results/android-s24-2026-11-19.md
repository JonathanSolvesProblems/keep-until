# Android on my Galaxy S24, 2026-11-19

The debug build of `android/` on my own Samsung Galaxy S24, over Wi-Fi, reading the built-in sample
photos (the broth carton's front, then its top). Screenshots taken on the phone:
`devpost/gallery/src/android-s24-turn.png` and `android-s24-keep.png` (gallery slide 11).

| Photo | Answer | How it was read, as the app shows it |
|---|---|---|
| Carton front (name, no date) | Turn it, so the date faces the camera | "Read in the cloud in 2.4 s, this phone has no on-device model" |
| Carton top (`2027 FE 14`) | Keep until 14 FEB 2029 (Food Banks Canada chart, page 8) | "Read twice in the cloud in 3.9 s, this phone has no on-device model" |

The Galaxy S24 is not on Google's list of phones that run Gemini Nano through ML Kit (Samsung support
starts at the S25, `devpost/sources.md`), so the app takes the cloud path and says so on the answer.
The shift list shows the item as "Campbell's Chicken Broth (sample)". Samples are never written to a
food bank's log.

Not measured here: on-device reading on Android (no supported phone was available).

## Offline on the same phone, 2026-11-24

With Wi-Fi and mobile data switched off (`dumpsys connectivity`: "Active default network: none"),
a sample read fails and the phone says: "No connection. This phone reads dates in the cloud, so
reading waits for Wi-Fi. Items already read stay in this shift's list." The shift was started
online before the connection was cut; starting a shift offline was checked only on the Android 8
emulator. Earlier the same day the full sample walk-through ran online on this phone: turn it
(2.2 s), keep until 14 FEB 2029 (4.4 s for both reads), set aside for the yogurt (2.1 s).

Found in this check and fixed: after a failed read, the sample photo stayed on screen above the
previous item's answer. The photo is now taken down when a read fails.
