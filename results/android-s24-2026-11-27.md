## What went wrong, and what changed

- **A confirming read that never came back.** At 20:37 the first read of the jar found 14/03/2021 in
  3.5 s. The second, independent read ran for about 100 seconds and failed with the SDK's own message,
  "Something unexpected happened." The gate did its job: no discard on one read, the jar went to the
  lead. But the screen showed that SDK message, and a volunteer would have waited 100 seconds.
  Neither platform capped a read's length or its time. The likely cause is a read that kept writing
  until its JSON broke, the way Gemini Nano repeated digits in `results/nano-alone-2026-11-27.md`; I
  did not capture the raw reply, so this is not proven. Changed on web and Android: at most 1,024
  output tokens per read (a read is about 150) and a 30-second request timeout, so a stuck read fails
  in seconds; the screen now says "the second read did not come back, so a person checks it before
  anything is binned", and the record keeps the error. One failure in five runs is too few to say how
  often it happens.
- **A doubled name.** At 20:46 the shift list showed "Campbell's Campbell's Chicken Broth". The front
  photo's brand and product were joined without the dedupe the list uses, sent into the top photo's
  prompt as "what earlier photos showed", and copied back by the model. Both platforms now build the
  name once (`printedName`, tested in `web/src/ai/loop.test.ts` and `PrintedNameTest.kt`).

## The screen in these runs

Read item and the sample button are pinned to the bottom of the screen. While a read is out the item
being read is in view, the print head runs along the button and the last answer is dimmed; when the
answer lands its colour wipes across the panel, the word sets, the date prints column by column, the
phone pulses (one firm pulse for keep, two light ticks for set aside, a long buzz for discard), and
the shift's tallies count up.
